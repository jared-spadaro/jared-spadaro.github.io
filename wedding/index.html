Our story

We got to know each other as friends for several months before we began dating. We had instant chemistry. Our similar senses of humor formed the bedrock of our time together. In an early ploy to allow him to spend some extra quality time with Hannah, Jared attended a yoga class for the first time in his life.

We share a love and appreciation of nature. A common weekend activity in the summer months is a hike in the coastal hills of Mt Tamalpais or the redwoods of the Santa Cruz mountains. Alternatively, we will often stroll around the hills of San Francisco, frequenting its many serene parks and listening to the chattering of the local birds. In the winters, our favorite activity is snowboarding at Palisades Tahoe. A typical weekday evening consists of cooking dinner together and going for an evening walk to talk about our days.

Timeline

  1. August 2019

    It begins

    We met at Cisco Systems in San Jose CA, our first jobs out of college. This where we got know each other amid the sea of computer screens and the gurgling utterances of the CEO's old coffee machine (we got recruited by the office manager to help care for the old beast).

  2. 2020 - 2022

    A Tale of Two Cities

    For the first several years, Jared made a weekly pilgrimage from San Jose to San Francisco to spend weekends with Hannah. Many thanks are due to Hannah's roommates for allowing him to be an itinerant lodger during this period.

  3. June 2022

    Head in the Clouds

    Jared made the fateful decision to move to San Francisco to be closer to Hannah, in the pint-sized neighborhood of Diamond Heights. The area is best known for its lofty location above the city, which brings exposure to a combination of high winds and thick fog.

  4. August 2023

    Den of thieves

    We moved in together in the Noe Valley neighborhood of San Francisco in the second floor of a century-old duplex perched atop a hill just a block from the famous Mission Dolores Park. The location is notable for its proximity to several well-known neighborhoods, including Castro and Mission.

  5. August 2025

    Stepping up

    After the first attempt went awry a day earlier, Jared successfully proposed to Hannah at the Pemberton Steps, a series of stunning stairwells draped in greenery in the hills of San Francisco. We often walk up them during our regularly scheduled city excursions.

  6. July 17, 2027

    Onward

    We couldn't be more thrilled to celebrate the start of the next chapter of our lives with all of you in beautiful Salem Massachusetts!

Travel

Where to stay

A room block at the Hawthrone hotel will open about six months before the wedding, booking link will be posted here. There are several accomodation options directly in downtown Salem:

  • Hawthorne Hotel
  • The Hotel Salem
  • The Merchant
  • Salem Waterfront Hotel
  • The Salem Inn
  • The Stepping Stone Inn
  • Alternatively, there are rentals available via Airbnb etc in Salem and nearby Marblehead

Getting there

  • Fly into Boston Logan Airport (BOS) or take the train to South Station.
  • Uber or Lyft to Salem from Logan: 30–40 minutes.
  • The Newburyport/Rockport commuter rail goes from North Station to Salem. To get to North Station from Logan, take a car (~5 mins).
  • A rental car will allow you to explore the scenic North Shore area, however Salem is easily navigable by Uber and Lyft.
