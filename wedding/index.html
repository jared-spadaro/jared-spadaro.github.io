Our Story

Hannah spends her days building technology that keeps wildfires from starting. Jared spends his building worlds in augmented reality. Somewhere between protecting the real one and inventing new ones, they found each other — and a life that sounds, most evenings, like Jared’s guitar drifting through the apartment while Hannah pretends she didn’t ask for that song again.

    Where it began

    We met at Cisco Systems in San Jose CA, our first jobs out of college.

  2. January 2020

    Things heat up

    A memorable early date was a beautiful hike in Big Basin Redwoods State Park (sadly, it has since been severely burned by a wildfire).

  3. January 2023

    We love to snowboard together. Our home mountain is Palisades Tahoe.

    Den of thieves

Getting to Salem

Travel

Getting here

Fly into Boston Logan (BOS). Salem is about 30–40 minutes north by Uber or Lyft. Or take the T to North Station and ride the Newburyport/Rockport commuter rail to Salem — about half an hour — then walk a few minutes to the museum.

Getting around

Salem is wonderfully walkable: the museum, the waterfront and most hotels are a few blocks apart. A rental car opens up the rest of the North Shore — Marblehead’s harbor, Rockport and Gloucester on Cape Ann, Crane Beach in Ipswich — and gets you to Friday’s bonfire in Swampscott.
