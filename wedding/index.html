Our Story

We got to know each other as friends for several months before any romantic element emerged. This allowed us to get acquainted without the pressure of a relationship. As friends, we had instant chemistry. Our similar senses of humor formed the bedrock of our time together. In an early ploy to allow him to spend some extra quality time with Hannah, Jared attended a yoga class for the first time in his life.

We share a love and appreciation of nature. A common weekend activity in the summer months is a hike in the coastal hills of Mt Tamalapais or the redwoods of the Santa Cruz mountains. Alternatively, we will often stroll around the hills of San Francisco, frequenting its many bucolic parks and listening to the chattering of the local birds. In the winters, our favorite activity is snowboarding at Palisades Tahoe. Amongst sweeping mountain vistas, we always experience a vast range of emotions as we navigate dynamic weather conditions and varied terrain.

  1. August 2019

    It begins

    We met at Cisco Systems in San Jose CA, our first jobs out of college. This where we got know each other amid the sea of computer screens and the gurgling utterances of the CEO's old coffee machine (we got recruited by the office manager to help care for the old beast).

  2. 2020 - 2022

    A Tale of Two Cities

    For the first several years, Jared made a weekly pilgrimage from San Jose to San Francisco to spend weekends with his beaux. Many thanks are due to Hannah's roommates for allowing him to be an itinerant lodger during this period.

  3. June 2022

    Head in the Clouds

    Jared made the fateful decision to move to San Francisco, in the pint-sized neighborhood of Diamond Heights. The area is best known for its lofty location above the city, which brings exposure to a combination of high winds and thick fog.

  4. August 2023

    Den of thieves

    We moved in together in the Noe Valley neighborhood of San Francisco in the second floor of a century-old duplex perched atop a hill just a block from the famous Mission Dolores Park. The location is notable for its proximity to several well-known neighborhoods, including Castro and Mission.

  5. August 2025

    A cut above

    Jared popped the question at the Pemberton Steps, a series of stunning stairwells draped in greenery in the hills of San Francisco. We often walk up them during our regularly scheduled city excursions.

  6. July 17, 2027

    Sailor's Knot

    We couldn't be more thrilled to celebrate the start of the next chapter of our lives with all of you in beautiful Salem Massachusetts!

Getting to Salem

Travel

Getting here

Fly into Boston Logan (BOS). Salem is about 30–40 minutes north by Uber or Lyft. Or take the T to North Station and ride the Newburyport/Rockport commuter rail to Salem — about half an hour — then walk a few minutes to the museum.

Getting around

Salem is wonderfully walkable: the museum, the waterfront and most hotels are a few blocks apart. A rental car opens up the rest of the North Shore — Marblehead’s harbor, Rockport and Gloucester on Cape Ann, Crane Beach in Ipswich — and gets you to Friday’s bonfire in Swampscott.
